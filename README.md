## Our Approach

OneLoop's positioning is built into its name: growth breaks down in the gaps — between a business's website, its marketing, its operations, and its brand — usually because each piece is handled by a different vendor with no one owning the outcome. We close that loop.

The way we work with every client follows the same cycle:

1. **Identify** — find the specific problem holding a business back, not a generic audit checklist
2. **Understand** — dig into why it's happening, not just what's visible on the surface
3. **Build** — ship the website, campaign, automation, or system that solves it
4. **Scale** — keep optimizing month over month as the business grows, rather than handing off a project and disappearing

## Proven Results

Real, verified Google Ads performance across four live client accounts — the campaign data behind each result is available on request.

### RMC Plumbing Services — Australia
*Local services lead generation · Google Ads (Search) · Client since October 2023*

**The Challenge:** RMC Plumbing needed a steady, predictable flow of local service calls and job requests from Google Ads, at a cost per lead that kept the campaign profitable as they scaled spend.

**The Results:**
- Leads grew from 11/month to 66/month — a **6.1x** increase
- Cost per lead dropped from $92 to $19 — a **79%** reduction
- Since onboarding: 1,327 leads delivered on AUD $32,499 in total ad spend (≈$24 average cost per lead across the full engagement)

**How We Got There:**
- Rebuilt campaign structure around high-intent, service-specific search terms instead of broad-match generic keywords
- Continuously trimmed underperforming keywords and search terms to lift click-through rate and Quality Score, pulling average CPC down over time
- Shifted budget toward the times of day and campaign types converting at the lowest cost per lead
- Ongoing monthly testing of ad copy and offers to keep conversion rate climbing as the account matured

### Bamboo Spa — New Zealand
*Wellness & local bookings · Google Ads (Search) · Client since October 2024*

**The Challenge:** Bamboo Spa runs on a high volume of small, everyday booking actions — calls, form fills, directions requests — so the priority was driving more of these local conversions each month while keeping cost per conversion low enough to scale spend comfortably.

**The Results:**
- Conversions grew from 1,237/month to 2,587/month — a **2.1x** increase
- Cost per conversion dropped from $1.44 to $0.78 — a **46%** reduction
- Total since onboarding: 40,624 conversions delivered on NZD $38,778 in ad spend

**How We Got There:**
- Expanded coverage across the full range of local booking and enquiry actions customers take, not just phone calls
- Tightened geo and audience targeting to focus spend on the areas driving real footfall
- Used performance data month over month to keep shifting budget toward the highest-converting campaigns

### Bikers Wear Australia — Australia
*E-commerce — motorcycle apparel & gear · Google Ads (Shopping & Search) · Client since April 2024*

**The Challenge:** An established e-commerce account, already running profitably, needed to scale ad spend without losing return on ad spend (ROAS) — and to do it on cleanly-tracked, trustworthy conversion data.

**The Results (most recent 17 months, Mar 2025–Jul 2026):**
- Monthly spend scaled from AUD $4,331 to AUD $5,785
- Monthly revenue grew from AUD $39,886 to AUD $51,394
- ROAS held steady at approximately **9x** throughout
- AUD $753,330 in revenue generated on AUD $88,017 of ad spend across the full window

**How We Got There:**
- Restructured Shopping campaigns around product margin and sell-through, not just click volume
- Cleaned up and re-validated conversion tracking so bidding decisions were based on trustworthy data
- Used Performance Max and audience signals to find incremental, high-value customers as budget scaled up

### Bikers Gear UK — United Kingdom
*E-commerce — motorcycle apparel & gear · Google Ads (Shopping & Search) · Client since January 2025*

**The Challenge:** Similar brief to its Australian counterpart — keep the account profitable through a conversion-tracking clean-up, and hold a healthy ROAS as the market and account matured.

**The Results (most recent 10 months, Oct 2025–Jul 2026):**
- £123,940 in revenue generated on £26,723 in ad spend
- A blended ROAS of **4.64x**, holding consistently in the 4–5x range month after month

**How We Got There:**
- Re-mapped and verified conversion actions to get bidding decisions onto reliable data
- Kept campaign structure aligned to the AU account's proven approach while adapting for the UK market
- Held ROAS steady through the tracking transition — the platform for scaling spend going forward
